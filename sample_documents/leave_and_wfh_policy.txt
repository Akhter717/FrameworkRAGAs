Leave and Work From Home Policy

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per year and 10 sick leaves per year. Casual leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year, but up to 5 unused sick leaves may be carried forward. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal, except in medical emergencies.

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior approval from their reporting manager. Full-time remote work requires written approval from the department head and HR. Employees working from home must be available on company communication tools during standard working hours, which are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
